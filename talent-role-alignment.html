The challenge

Skills and experience don't tell the whole story.

CVs, interviews, and performance history show what someone has done. They reveal much less about how a person naturally works, contributes, makes decisions, and where their potential may fit best. That gap affects hiring, internal mobility, and redeployment decisions.

PathKEY adds a deeper layer of human insight to talent decisions, helping organizations understand the person, the role, and the alignment between them.

What it delivers

See fit from more than one angle.

Bring deeper human insight into talent decisions by looking beyond skills and experience to how people are naturally equipped to work, contribute, and grow.
