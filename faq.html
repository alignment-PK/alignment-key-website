About PathKEY

The basics of what PathKEY is and who it's for.

What is PathKEY?+
PathKEY is a Human Potential Intelligence Platform designed to help people and organizations understand human potential more deeply and turn that understanding into practical decisions.

It brings together multiple perspectives — including career interests, personality, natural tendencies and contextual information — and connects them through structured methodology and responsible AI.

PathKEY is used across career development, coaching, talent and role alignment, team development and future-of-work conversations.
Is PathKEY an assessment?+
Assessment is part of PathKEY, but PathKEY is broader than a single assessment.

Rather than relying on one framework or one perspective, PathKEY brings together different kinds of insight and connects them into a multidimensional view of the person. Those insights can then be applied to different questions — from career direction and development to talent, roles and teams.
What makes PathKEY different from traditional assessments?+
Many assessments are designed to examine a particular dimension — personality, interests, strengths or another aspect of the individual.

PathKEY brings multiple perspectives together. It combines established frameworks such as RIASEC and the Big Five with selected Human Design for Business insights and contextual information.

The goal is not to produce one definitive "truth" about a person, but to create a richer set of insights from which better questions, conversations and decisions can emerge.
Who is PathKEY for?+
PathKEY is designed for both individuals and professionals working with people.

Individuals can use PathKEY to explore career direction, strengths, working preferences and development. Coaches and consultants can use it to deepen their work with clients. Organizations can apply PathKEY to career development, outplacement, talent and role alignment, leadership and team development.

Methodology & Science

How PathKEY combines different frameworks responsibly.

What frameworks does PathKEY use?+
PathKEY brings together several complementary perspectives.

RIASEC provides a structured view of vocational interests. The Big Five adds insight into broad personality and behavioral tendencies. Selected Human Design for Business elements provide an additional, non-self-reported perspective on natural tendencies. Contextual information helps make those insights relevant to the person's situation and the question being explored.

Each perspective serves a different purpose and is processed through its own methodology before insights are connected.
Is PathKEY scientifically validated?+
There is an important distinction here.

PathKEY incorporates established frameworks such as RIASEC and the Big Five, which have substantial research foundations in vocational psychology and personality research.

PathKEY also incorporates Human Design for Business as a complementary interpretive perspective. Human Design does not have the same scientific evidence base as established psychological frameworks such as RIASEC and the Big Five, and we do not present it as a scientifically validated psychometric assessment.

We believe being clear about these distinctions is part of using different methodologies responsibly.
Why does PathKEY include Human Design for Business?+
Most assessments depend on how people respond to questions about themselves. That information can be valuable, but self-reporting is naturally influenced by experience, circumstances and self-perception.

Human Design for Business gives PathKEY an additional perspective that does not depend on questionnaire responses. We use selected elements to explore natural tendencies around areas such as contribution, decision-making, interaction and ways of working.

It does not replace psychological assessment, predict performance or determine what someone can or cannot become. It is used as a complementary perspective alongside other inputs.
Does PathKEY define what someone should do?+
No.

PathKEY is designed to expand understanding, not prescribe a person's future. It does not diagnose people, determine ability or decide which career, role or life choice someone must pursue.

Its purpose is to surface relevant patterns, possibilities and questions that can support better-informed decisions.

AI & Responsible Use

How AI supports PathKEY without replacing human judgment.

How does PathKEY use AI?+
AI helps PathKEY connect, contextualize and communicate complex information.

The underlying assessment results are not invented by AI. Inputs are first processed through defined scoring models, rules and methodologies. AI can then support the synthesis and contextualization of those insights for the question being explored.

We see AI as an intelligence layer that supports the process — not as a replacement for methodology or human judgment.
Does AI make decisions about people?+
No.

PathKEY is designed to support human decision-making, not automate it. AI does not decide whether someone should be hired, promoted, moved into a role or pursue a particular career.

Especially in organizational contexts, PathKEY insights should be considered alongside experience, skills, interviews, professional judgment and the wider context.

PathKEY should inform a decision — never become the decision.
How does PathKEY approach privacy?+
PathKEY is built in Europe with privacy by design and GDPR principles embedded into how personal information is handled.

We aim to collect only the information needed for the intended purpose and treat personal insight as information that deserves particular care.

Privacy, responsible AI, methodological transparency and human oversight are core principles in how we are building the platform.

PathKEY Career

Everything about the individual PathKEY Career Report.

What is the PathKEY Career Report?+
PathKEY Career is a personalized report designed to help people understand themselves more deeply in relation to work and career.

It explores areas such as career interests, personality and behavioral tendencies, strengths, natural ways of working, interaction and communication, decision-making and potential career directions.

Rather than giving you a single career answer, it provides multiple perspectives you can use to explore where and how you may thrive. A dedicated version is also available for students and young adults under 25.
How long does the PathKEY Career assessment take?+
The online assessment typically takes around 15–20 minutes.

Once the required information has been completed, your responses are processed to create your personalized PathKEY Career Report.
How long is the report?+
A PathKEY Career Report is typically 40+ pages, bringing together different dimensions of the individual's profile into one integrated view.

The exact content may vary depending on the report version and the sections included.
How quickly will I receive my report?+
PathKEY Career reports are normally delivered digitally within 24 hours after the required assessment and information have been completed.
Can I buy a PathKEY Career Report without working with a coach?+
Yes.

You can purchase your own PathKEY Career Report and explore it independently. Coaching is not required.

If you later want deeper interpretation or support translating your insights into career decisions or action, you can choose to work with a certified PathKEY professional.
How much does a PathKEY Career Report cost?+
A single personalized PathKEY Career Report is €150.

Certified PathKEY professionals access reports through the Partner Platform under separate professional pricing and report-pack arrangements.
Do PathKEY reports expire?+
No. Your report remains yours to return to.

Some of the perspectives it contains explore relatively stable tendencies, while the meaning you take from those insights can naturally evolve as your experience, circumstances and self-awareness change.

That is why the same report can often generate different questions at different stages of a person's career.

Coaches & Organizations

How professionals and organizations apply PathKEY.

Can I work with a PathKEY coach?+
Yes.

Certified PathKEY professionals work with individuals across career development, transitions, leadership, coaching and other professional development contexts.

Our Find a Coach directory can help you find a professional based on areas of expertise, location, language and the type of support you are looking for.
Are all PathKEY Certified Coaches ICF credentialed?+
No. PathKEY certification and an individual ICF credential are separate qualifications.

Some PathKEY professionals are ICF-credentialed coaches, while others come from consulting, HR, talent, learning and development, career development or related professional backgrounds.

Alignment Key's PathKEY Level 1 Certification program has been approved for 16 ICF Continuing Coach Education credits: 11 Core Competency and 5 Resource Development credits.
Can organizations use PathKEY?+
Yes. Organizational applications are an important part of the PathKEY platform.

PathKEY can support career navigation and reskilling, outplacement, talent and role alignment, coaching, leadership development and team development.

The objective is not simply to provide more assessment data, but to translate human insight into intelligence that can support better people decisions.
Can PathKEY be used for hiring or role decisions?+
PathKEY can provide an additional perspective for talent and role-alignment conversations, but it should not be used as a standalone hiring or employment decision tool.

Role fit is contextual. PathKEY insights should be considered alongside relevant experience, skills, interviews, organizational needs and professional judgment.
What is PathKEY Team?+
PathKEY Team brings individual PathKEY insights together into team-level Scenario Dashboards.

These dashboards help reveal patterns around contribution, communication, decision-making, collaboration and team dynamics that may be difficult to see when individual reports are viewed separately.

PathKEY Team is designed to support facilitated team development and scenario conversations rather than to rank or score teams.

PathKEY Team is currently in development / Beta.
What is Future of Work Mapping?+
Future of Work Mapping connects human potential with the changing world of work.

It is designed to help explore how roles, capabilities and career directions may evolve as AI and other changes reshape work — adding a future-facing perspective to career, talent and workforce conversations.

Future of Work Mapping is currently in Beta.

PathKEY AI

The AI-supported intelligence layer for certified professionals.

What is PathKEY AI?+
PathKEY AI is the AI-supported intelligence layer being developed within the PathKEY platform.

For certified professionals, it is designed to help translate PathKEY insights into practical coaching and development support — including deeper client insight, session preparation and contextualized interpretation.

The goal is to make sophisticated human insight easier to work with while keeping professional judgment and the coach or consultant at the center.
Does PathKEY AI replace the coach?+
No.

PathKEY AI is designed to make professionals better equipped, not unnecessary.

It can help process information, identify relevant connections and prepare for conversations, but it cannot replace the relationship, context, questioning, judgment and human presence that good coaching and consulting require.

PathKEY AI is currently in Beta.

Certification

The PathKEY Certification pathway for professionals.

Who is PathKEY Certification for?+
PathKEY Certification is designed for professionals who want to work more deeply with human potential.

Participants include coaches, career professionals, HR and talent professionals, consultants, learning and development professionals and others whose work involves helping people navigate careers, development, roles or organizational change.
How does the PathKEY Certification journey work?+
PathKEY Certification is designed as a progressive professional journey:

Level 1 — Certified Coach

Build the foundation for working with PathKEY at the individual level and translating report insights into meaningful development and coaching conversations.

Level 2 — Certified Consultant

Extend PathKEY into broader professional and organizational applications, using multiple frameworks and structured methodologies across workshops and organizational solutions.

Level 3 — Certified Pro-Consultant — Coming Next

Move into team intelligence and advanced organizational practice, including team-level patterns, Scenario Dashboards and facilitated team development.

Each level builds on the capabilities developed in the previous stage.
What will Level 3 include?+
Level 3 will take PathKEY from individual and organizational insight into the dynamics of teams.

Certified Pro-Consultants will learn how to bring individual PathKEY profiles together, interpret team-level patterns through Scenario Dashboards, and facilitate structured team development conversations and workshops.

The program is being designed for experienced PathKEY professionals who want to work more deeply with organizations and teams, including areas such as contribution, communication, decision-making, collaboration and changing team scenarios.
Do I have to complete Level 1 before Level 2?+
Yes. The certification journey is progressive, with each level building on the methodology and professional application developed at the previous level.

Level 1 provides the foundation for the PathKEY methodology and individual application before moving into more advanced organizational work.
Is the PathKEY Certification ICF-approved?+
Alignment Key's PathKEY Level 1 Certification program has been approved for 16 ICF Continuing Coach Education (CCE) credits:

11 Core Competency credits

5 Resource Development credits

This does not mean that PathKEY itself, the PathKEY network or every PathKEY Certified Coach is ICF-accredited or ICF-credentialed.
Is certification delivered live?+
Yes. PathKEY Certification includes live learning and professional application.

Live participation is an important part of the certification process because the program goes beyond learning report content. Participants develop interpretation and application skills through discussion, practice and interaction with trainers.

Recordings may be made available as learning support, but they do not replace the live-attendance requirements for certification.
Who delivers PathKEY Certification?+
Programs are delivered by approved PathKEY Trainers who have completed the required trainer preparation and certification process.

Different cohorts may be delivered in different languages and by different trainers. The trainer, language, schedule, price and availability for each open cohort are shown on the Upcoming Certifications page.
In which languages is PathKEY Certification available?+
Certification cohorts are offered in selected languages depending on trainer and cohort availability.

Current open programs, including their language and trainer, are always listed on the Upcoming Certifications page.
How much does certification cost?+
Pricing can vary by certification level, cohort and market.

Current pricing is shown directly on each cohort card on the Upcoming Certifications page, together with dates, trainer, language and registration details.

PathKEY for Students

PathKEY Career for students and young adults under 25.

What is PathKEY Career for Students?+
PathKEY Career for Students is a dedicated version of PathKEY Career designed for students and young adults who are exploring education, study and future career possibilities.

It helps them understand their interests, strengths, personality and behavioral tendencies, natural ways of working and learning, decision-making, and potential career directions.

The goal is not to tell a student what they should become, but to give them a richer understanding of themselves so they can explore their options with greater clarity.
What age group is the Student Report designed for?+
The PathKEY Career Student Report is designed for students and young adults under the age of 25.

The content and language are adapted to this stage of life, with a greater focus on self-discovery, education, career exploration and the choices students may be starting to make about their future.

For people aged 25 and over, PathKEY Career provides a version designed for adult career and professional contexts.
Can parents purchase a PathKEY Career Student Report for their child?+
Yes.

Parents can purchase a PathKEY Career Student Report to help their child explore their interests, strengths, ways of working and learning, and possible career directions.

For students under 18, parent or legal representative approval is required before the report can be created.

We encourage parents to use the report as a starting point for conversation rather than as a way to decide a young person's future for them. The goal is to help students understand themselves well enough to begin making their own informed choices.
Does PathKEY tell students which career they should choose?+
No.

PathKEY does not prescribe a career, predict a student's future or define what they can or cannot become.

Instead, it brings together different perspectives to highlight interests, strengths, natural tendencies and career areas that may be worth exploring further.

Career ideas in the report should be treated as possibilities to investigate, not answers to follow.
Can schools and education professionals use PathKEY with students?+
Yes.

Schools, counselors and education professionals can use PathKEY as part of career guidance and student development programs, either with individual students or with groups.

Each student receives personalized insight that can provide a structured starting point for more meaningful conversations about education, strengths, career interests and future possibilities.

Schools or education organizations interested in using PathKEY with groups of students can contact Alignment Key to discuss the most appropriate approach for their program.
