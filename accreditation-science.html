How it comes together

Different inputs. A structured path to insight.

PathKEY does not treat different frameworks as interchangeable, nor does it ask AI to invent meaning from raw data. Each input is processed through its own defined methodology before insights are connected across perspectives.

Responsible AI layer
Supports synthesis, contextualization and communication. AI supports the process. It does not replace the underlying scoring logic, defined methodology or human judgment.
01
Measure

Each perspective is processed on its own terms.

Career interests, personality traits and other inputs are first evaluated through their respective scoring models and defined rules.

RIASEC · Big Five · Human Design input · Context
02
Interpret

Data becomes structured insight.

Scores and defined outputs are translated through controlled logic and curated content into meaningful interpretations, while keeping the source and nature of each insight distinguishable.

Raw data → scores → interpreted outputs
03
Connect

Patterns are explored across perspectives.

PathKEY connects relevant insights across different dimensions to identify patterns, complementarities and tensions that may not be visible from one perspective alone.

Connections · New insights
04
Apply

Insight is translated into context.

The resulting intelligence is applied to the question at hand, from career direction and development to role alignment, coaching and team dynamics, with human judgment remaining part of the process.

Career · Coaching · Talent · Teams

The goal is not to produce one “truth” about a person. It is to create a richer set of insights and perspectives from which better questions, conversations and decisions can emerge.

The credibility behind our foundations

RIASEC

Established career interest framework
  • Built on more than six decades of vocational psychology research.
  • Widely used in career guidance, education and career development around the world.
  • Used by career professionals, educational institutions and organizations to connect interests with work environments and career direction.
What it gives PathKEY

A structured foundation for understanding the kinds of work, activities and environments a person is naturally drawn toward.

Big Five

Established personality framework
  • One of the most widely researched and established models of personality.
  • Studied across countries, cultures and languages over decades of research.
  • Widely used in psychological research, organizational settings and personality assessment.
What it gives PathKEY

A structured perspective on broad personality and behavioral tendencies, including how someone may approach work, interaction, change, structure and responsibility.

Human Design

Established experiential framework
  • Explored and applied by a global community for decades.
  • Practiced and experimented with by people around the world in different contexts.
  • Many who experiment with it report experiencing its insights as highly accurate and relevant to their lived experience.
What it gives PathKEY

A distinct perspective on natural tendencies, contribution, interaction, decision-making, energy and ways of working that is integrated with the other PathKEY inputs.

Different foundations. One integrated approach.

References & further reading

Explore the frameworks behind PathKEY.

For those who want to go deeper, these resources provide additional background on the established frameworks, professional standards and methodological perspectives referenced throughout PathKEY.

01 — RIASEC & vocational interests +

PathKEY uses the RIASEC framework as a structured approach to vocational interests. Developed through the work of John Holland, RIASEC describes six broad interest orientations — Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising and Conventional — and has been extensively applied in career exploration and occupational research. O*NET also uses RIASEC as the basis of its vocational interest model and publishes RIASEC profiles for occupations.

Suggested references
  • Holland, J. L. (1997). Making Vocational Choices: A Theory of Vocational Personalities and Work Environments.
  • O*NET Interest Profiler — technical & research documentation
  • Rounds, J. et al. — Updating Vocational Interests Information for the O*NET Content Model
  • Development of Occupational Interest Profiles for O*NET
02 — Big Five personality model +

The Big Five is a widely researched framework for describing broad patterns of personality across five dimensions: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness and Neuroticism / Emotional Stability. PathKEY uses Big Five results as one structured behavioral perspective within its broader methodology.

An important distinction: the scientific foundation of the Big Five model and the psychometric validation of the specific Big Five question set used inside PathKEY are not the same claim. The references below relate to framework research; PathKEY implementation documentation will be published separately as it becomes available.

03 — Human Design for Business +

PathKEY uses selected Human Design for Business principles as a complementary, non-self-reported perspective on natural tendencies in professional contexts. Human Design does not have the same scientific evidence base as RIASEC or the Big Five and is not presented by Alignment Key as a scientifically validated psychological assessment.

Further reading

Human Design for Business / BG5 methodology and professional applications.

Within PathKEY, Human Design-derived insights are used as complementary perspectives and are not used to diagnose, predict performance or determine a person’s capabilities.
04 — Professional coaching standards +

Alignment Key’s PathKEY Level 1 Certification program carries 16 ICF Continuing Coach Education credits. ICF Continuing Coach Education is designed for ongoing professional development and credential renewal for coaches, and is divided into two categories: Core Competencies and Resource Development.

  • 11 CCE — Core Competency
  • 5 CCE — Resource Development
  • 16 CCE — Total
