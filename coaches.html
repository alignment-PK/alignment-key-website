Your PathKEY Certification Journey

Start with individual potential. Grow into organizational impact.

PathKEY Certification is designed as a professional journey. Start by learning how to work with PathKEY insights in individual coaching, then take the next step by turning those insights into workshops and development experiences for teams and organizations.

Level 1

PathKEY Certified Coach

Understand individual potential and turn insight into meaningful action.

Learn the PathKEY methodology and how to use PathKEY insights in one-to-one coaching, career development and career transition. Build the confidence to interpret individual potential, guide meaningful conversations and turn insight into practical next steps.

You will learn to work with

PathKEY Career · Individual potential · Career direction · Coaching conversations · PathKEY AI

Best suited for

Coaches, career and outplacement professionals, HR professionals and Human Design professionals who want to work with individuals.

Level 2

PathKEY Certified Consultant

Take PathKEY from individual coaching into teams and organizations.

Build on Level 1 and learn how to design and facilitate workshops using PathKEY insights. You will work with two flexible workshop frameworks that can be adapted into a wide range of experiences based on the needs of a team or organization.

Through practical exercises, case studies and workshop planning, you will learn how to move beyond individual coaching and create meaningful group experiences around human potential, collaboration and development.

You will learn to

Design and structure PathKEY workshops · Adapt workshops to different organizational needs · Facilitate groups and teams · Use exercises and case studies effectively · Turn individual insights into meaningful group conversations

Best suited for

Certified PathKEY Coaches who want to expand their practice beyond one-to-one coaching and work as consultants with teams and organizations.

Level 3

PathKEY Certified Pro-Consultant

Team & Advanced Organizational Practice

Coming next

Level 3 takes PathKEY from individual and organizational insight into the dynamics of teams. Pro-Consultants learn how to bring individual PathKEY profiles together, interpret team-level patterns through Scenario Dashboards, and facilitate structured team development conversations and workshops.

The program is designed for experienced PathKEY professionals who want to work more deeply with organizations, helping leaders and teams explore contribution, communication, decision-making, collaboration and changing team scenarios using PathKEY Team and a ready-to-use facilitation methodology.
