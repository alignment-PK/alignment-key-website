THE CHALLENGE

A snapshot is useful. Human potential is bigger than a snapshot.

Most assessments rely primarily on how people describe themselves at a particular moment. They can provide valuable insight, but often leave important questions unanswered: What comes naturally? What may not be visible in self-perception? How might someone contribute in a different role, team, or future of work?

For individuals, this can limit the choices they see. For coaches and organizations, it can mean making important career, talent, and development decisions from only part of the picture.

The challenge is not getting more data. It is seeing enough of the person to make better decisions about what comes next.
